Jobs lose their number, and paperwork cannot be filed against them

Planned

A job created from a won quotation starts without a job number, documents cannot be attached to the job they belong to, nothing enforces what a job must contain, and the intake queue is a dead end.

Month-end figures cannot be trusted at a glance

Planned

One shipment can report two different profit figures, and the month-end documents are hidden inside an export dialog rather than being where month-end work happens.

Payments are recorded by hand, and foreign currency is guessed

Planned

Settling an invoice means retyping figures outside the system, and a payment in another currency posts a gain or loss that never happened.

Shipping documents can be released without the right checks

Planned

A bill of lading can be marked released or held without going through release control, can be rewritten while under review, and scanned values are accepted without flagging what failed validation.

Tracking and haulage sit apart from the job

Planned

Customs and port tracking cannot be seen from the shipment it belongs to, the customs declaration itself lives outside the system, trucks are not linked to jobs, and a shipment can never be closed.
